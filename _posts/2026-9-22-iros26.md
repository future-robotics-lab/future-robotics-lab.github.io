FuTURE Lab had a great time at the IEEE/RSJ International Conference on Intelligent Robots and Systems (IROS) 2026 in Pittsburgh! PhD student Aabha Tamhankar presented our latest work on contact-aware path planning for autonomous neuroendovascular navigation, and our PI Giovanni Pittiglio joined the panel "New Experiences in Clinical Collaborations: Junior Faculty in Medical Robotics" at the Training the Future Medical Roboticist workshop.
