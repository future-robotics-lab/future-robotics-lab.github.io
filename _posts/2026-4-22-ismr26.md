FuTURE Lab presented two papers at the International Symposium on Medical Robotics (ISMR) 2026! Our PI Giovanni Pittiglio, PhD student Aabha Tamhankar, and undergraduate researcher Alex Sayres shared our latest work on autonomous robots for stroke care and on the design of magnetic continuum robots.
